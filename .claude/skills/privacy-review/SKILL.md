# Privacy review

- What personal data is collected, where stored, who can read it
- Logs and traces must not include secrets or raw PII
- Deletion/export path if the product already has one
- Third parties (MCP, SaaS) treated as untrusted processors

Record findings in `sdlc/review.md`. This skill is guidance, not a legal opinion.
